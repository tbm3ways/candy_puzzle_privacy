Privacy Policy for Candy Puzzle
Last Updated: 13.01.2026

Candy Puzzle ("we", "our", or "us") provides this Privacy Policy to inform you of our policies and procedures regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information we receive from users of our mobile game.

1. Information Collection and Use
Our primary goal in collecting information is to provide and improve our Game and to provide you with a better experience.

Personal Data: We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, address, or email) unless you voluntarily provide it to us (e.g., via support emails).

Non-Personal Data: We may collect non-personal information such as device type, operating system version, and basic gameplay statistics to improve the game performance.

2. Third-Party Services (Ads and Analytics)
To keep the game free, we may use third-party advertising or analytics services. These services may collect information used to identify your device:

Google AdMob / AdSense: For displaying advertisements.

Unity Ads: For in-game rewarded videos.

Google Analytics for Firebase: To understand how players interact with the game.

These third parties have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

3. Children’s Privacy
Candy Puzzle is a family-friendly game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete such information from our files immediately.

4. Data Security
We prioritize the security of your data. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Permissions
The game may request the following permissions for specific functions:

Internet Access: To load puzzles and display ads.

Vibration: For haptic feedback during gameplay.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
